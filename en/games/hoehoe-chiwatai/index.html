HoeHoe ChiwaTai

Cute chihuahua barking action

One morning, the town's sky filled up with "gloomies".

Ohagi the chihuahua and friends bark them away! Just touch to move; barking is automatic. Pick a new skill at every level up, and walk for five minutes to clear the sky.

Features

  • 1

    Bark them away

    Just touch to move; barking is automatic. Gloomies leave dog biscuits behind. Pick them up to level up.

  • 2

    Pick one of three

    Each level up, choose one of three skills: bark rings, frisbees, bubbles, balloons and 30+ more. They change at levels 5 and 10.

  • 3

    All together: woof!

    Friends join your walk, and everyone can bark together for a big move.

  • 4

    6 chapters, 30 courses

    The park, the river, the beach and more, with a picture-book story. Hold out against Big Overcast until the sky clears, and try hard mode.

    Nothing scary

    Gloomies are fluffy dust and clouds with slightly pouty faces. The dogs never get hurt; they just look a little sad when bumped.

  • 6

    Grow and gear up

    Grow your skills and stack 24 pieces of gear. Dog friendship levels, missions and a daily bonus too.

How to play

  1. Touch the screen to move your dog. Barking is automatic.
  2. Cleared gloomies leave dog biscuits. Pick them up to level up.
  3. Each level up, choose one of three skills.
  4. Walk for five minutes before your hearts run out and the sky clears!

The dogs

Everyone except Ohagi joins as you clear courses. Each dog has a strength and a skill of its own.

  • Ohagi

    Chihuahua × Papillon

    Keen ears: a slightly wider bark.

  • Shiratama

    Chihuahua × Maltese

    One more heart (a little slower).

  • Kinako

    Chihuahua × Pomeranian

    Fluffy: stays safe longer after a bump.

  • Yokan

    Chihuahua × Dachshund

    Trotter: quick on its feet.

  • Dorayaki

    Chihuahua × Toy Poodle

    Good nose: picks up biscuits from afar.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, you can play to the end for free. There are in-app purchases and draws (see the next question).

How do the draws and purchases work?

Gear and Skill Draws use acorns earned by playing. Special Draws use Rainbow Gems, which you can buy or earn from level ups, missions and course clears (free gems are spent first).

Every draw shows its odds and its guaranteed draw count before you draw. 80 Special Draws let you pick any 5-star of that draw. No complete-the-set prizes, and no subscriptions.

Is it OK for children?

It's rated 9+ (because of the draws). We ask your age before your first purchase, and players under 20 have a monthly limit. On a child's device you can restrict purchases with Screen Time.

Are there ads?

Video ads only when you tap "watch" (more walk points, or double acorns on the results; up to five a day). No ads appear on their own. The No-Ads Pass gives the rewards without a video.

Where is my progress saved?

Automatically on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and bought gems can't be brought back. The No-Ads Pass comes back with "Restore purchases" in the shop.

Do you use AI?

The dogs' pictures come from 3D models made with Meshy, and the story pages, draw banners and icon were made with Google Gemini, then chosen and adjusted by the developer. The code and in-game text were written with Claude Code and reviewed by the developer. No AI content is generated while you play. Music (OpenTracks) and sound effects (Koukaon Lab, Kenney) are human-made.

Privacy in short

  • The developer collects no personal information, and there are no accounts. Progress stays on your device.
  • The video ad service, Google AdMob, may collect device information, an approximate region and device identifiers. The app doesn't ask for tracking permission, so this isn't used to track you across other apps.
  • Purchases go through the App Store; your payment details never reach the developer. Your age answer stays on your device.
